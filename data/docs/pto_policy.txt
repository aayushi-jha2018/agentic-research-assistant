Paid Time Off Policy

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, credited monthly. Unused PTO of up to 5 days may be carried over into the following year; any additional unused days are forfeited. Employees must request PTO at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three consecutive days. Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and does not require advance notice. New employees become eligible to use accrued PTO after completing 90 days of employment.
