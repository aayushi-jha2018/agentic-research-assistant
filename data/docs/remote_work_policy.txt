Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to four days per week, provided their manager approves the arrangement in writing. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, which run from 10 AM to 4 PM in their local time zone. Any equipment provided by the company, such as laptops or monitors, must be returned if employment ends. Employees working from a country other than their home country for more than 30 consecutive days must notify HR in advance for tax and compliance reasons. Home office stipends of up to $500 per year are available to cover the cost of a desk, chair, or monitor.
