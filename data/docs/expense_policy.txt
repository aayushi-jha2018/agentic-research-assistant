Expense Reimbursement Policy

All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date, accompanied by an itemized receipt. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Airfare should be booked in economy class unless the flight duration exceeds six hours, in which case premium economy is permitted. Expenses submitted without a receipt will only be reimbursed if the amount is under $25. Reimbursements are processed within two pay cycles after approval by a manager.
